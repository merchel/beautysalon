Our Salon Story

Beauty Care With Soft Precision

Aurora Beauty Lounge was created for clients who want more than a quick appointment. Our Bellevue studio blends personalized consultation, licensed beauty professionals, premium products, and modern beauty techniques in a calm lounge setting.
